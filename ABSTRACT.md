# Abstract

This is an external, AI-assisted audit of OpenAI's manuscript "The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis: A Zero-Free Half-Plane Re(s) > 7/8" (September 30, 2026; openai/math commit adc7f12) and its Lean formalization, not an independent re-proof of the analytic estimates. It combines a layer-by-layer reconstruction of the argument, an audit of the paper's own exponent accounting, numerical evaluation of the critical constants, and an outline of how the Lean statements line up with the logical hinge. The residual risk is quantitative: the margins are small (1/4800 in Stage 1, 49/440640 > 1/10000 at the Stage 2 endpoint), and ruling out an unlisted intermediate loss larger than the reserved budgets requires a specialist reading of the original manuscript. Within that scope, the architecture is coherent at every examined layer, and the Stage 1 and Stage 2 ledgers close with a positive remainder under the losses the paper explicitly charges. The Lean statements of the final theorems match the paper; a third-party kernel replay is reported for the zeta statement, and we built no Lean ourselves. No structural gap or disproof was found. A loss table, residual-risk notes and a prioritized review request let specialists decide quickly where to read.
