# Abstract

This is an external audit of OpenAI's manuscript "The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis: A Zero-Free Half-Plane Re(s) > 7/8" (September 30, 2026) and its Lean formalization, not an independent re-proof of the analytic estimates. It combines a layer-by-layer reconstruction of the argument, a specialist audit of the paper's own exponent accounting, computational confirmation of the critical constants, and a Lean skeleton of the logical hinge. The residual risk is quantitative: the margins are small (1/4800 in Stage 1, 49/440640 > 1/10000 at the Stage 2 endpoint), and confirming that no intermediate lemma hides an unlisted loss larger than the reserved budgets requires a specialist reading of the original manuscript. Within that scope, the logical architecture is coherent at every examined layer; the paper's Stage 1 and Stage 2 ledgers close with a positive remainder under the losses they explicitly charge; the row envelope R(δ) and the endpoint constant match under direct evaluation; and the Lean statements of the final theorems match the claimed theorem and are machine-checked. No structural gap or disproof was found. We provide a loss table, residual-risk notes and a prioritized expert-review request so specialists can decide quickly where to read.
